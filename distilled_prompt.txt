## 1. Problem Statement

A payment company records customer transactions instantly on its payment platform. However, the bank settles the money after 1–2 days.

At the end of the month, the company's internal payment records and bank settlement records should match.

The application must compare both datasets and identify discrepancies.

It must detect exactly these three required discrepancy categories:

1. Rounding Difference: Small transaction-level differences that become noticeable when amounts are summed.
2. Duplicate Entry: A transaction recorded more than once in either dataset.
3. Unmatched Refund: A refund transaction without a corresponding original transaction.

Also identify transactions that are not yet settled because the bank settlement can take 1–2 days. Do not incorrectly classify these as errors.

## 5. Core Reconciliation Engine

This is the most important part of the project.

Build a deterministic reconciliation engine using Python and Pandas.

Do not use an LLM to make the actual financial matching decisions.

### A. Data validation

- Check mandatory columns.
- Validate amount fields.
- Validate dates.
- Check missing transaction IDs.
- Check supported currencies.
- Handle invalid or empty CSV files.
- Show clear error messages.

### B. Transaction matching

Match platform transactions with bank settlements using transaction_id, currency, amount and settlement information.

Handle settlement delays of 1–2 days.

Classify transactions into:
- Matched.
- Pending Settlement.
- Amount Mismatch.
- Missing from Bank.
- Missing from Platform.

Do not treat a transaction as missing from the bank if it is still within the configured settlement window.

### C. Duplicate detection

Detect duplicate transaction IDs separately in the platform dataset and bank dataset.

Show:
- Duplicate transaction ID.
- Number of occurrences.
- Dataset where duplication occurred.
- Total financial impact where it can be calculated reliably.

Do not silently remove duplicates before reporting them.

### D. Rounding difference detection

Detect small amount differences between platform and bank records.

Support configurable rounding tolerance.

Also calculate aggregate differences by currency and settlement period so that small individual differences that appear insignificant can be identified when summed.

Clearly distinguish:
- Individual amount differences.
- Cumulative rounding differences.

### E. Unmatched refund detection

For every refund, verify whether its original_transaction_id exists in the platform payment records.

Identify:
- Valid refunds.
- Refunds with missing original transactions.
- Refunds with invalid references.
- Refunds with inconsistent amounts, where applicable.

Do not incorrectly classify a valid refund as an unmatched refund.
